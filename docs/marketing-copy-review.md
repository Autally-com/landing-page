# Hotel pilot messaging review

Reviewed 23 September 2026. This is a positioning and copy review, not evidence of measured conversion improvement.

## Positioning decision

Lead with a recognizable hotel invoice problem, then explain the shared exchange immediately below the hero. Hotels and property management systems are the pilot audience. The company that needs the invoice is the ultimate beneficiary. Autally is developing the exchange between systems, not replacing a hotel PMS or an expense application.

Primary action: **Explore a pilot**, opening an email to tomas@autally.com. The contact section explains what to send and what the discussion will cover. This invites a conversation without implying acceptance into a working network.

## Evaluation of the supplied feedback

| Feedback | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- |
| Keep a hotel-specific opening | Adopted. A concrete initial use case is easier to understand than an abstract infrastructure claim. |
| Lead with effortless reconciliation | Adapted. “Less admin after checkout” speaks to hotel teams; the diagram and example explain the company's expense and reconciliation outcome. “Effortless” would overstate an unproven pilot. |
| Explain the network and both sides in the hero | Adopted. Hotel systems, expense and accounting apps are named, alongside the development stage. |
| Use “For the company” | Adopted in both diagrams. Clearer than procurement-oriented “business buyer.” |
| Broaden the story directly after the hero | Adopted as a visible heading: “Hotels first. An exchange built for more.” |
| Use “Explore a pilot” | Adopted consistently in navigation, hero and contact. |
| Show the larger infrastructure vision | Adopted. The network section names future POS, receipt, payment and banking participation; route examples distinguish pilot focus from future possibilities. |

## Sales clarity and structure

1. Hotel outcome and exchange explanation.
2. Immediate bridge to the broader vision.
3. Concrete payment-versus-invoice example and the company benefit.
4. Provider benefits: fewer follow-ups, reusable connections, possible retrieval compensation.
5. Broader network and objections: competing applications, current readiness.
6. A clear next step by email.

Provider compensation remains exploratory, applies to the fulfilling provider, and has no published rate or guaranteed reward. Participating receipt apps can both provide and retrieve documents, extend coverage and retain their own customer experience. No candidate organizations, unverified partners, private financial assumptions or unsupported performance claims were added.

The established fonts, mint palette, glass surfaces and animations are retained. The broader vision has a stronger heading, ordinary body copy is more readable, FAQ paragraphs have a bounded measure, and mobile diagram qualifiers are larger.
